## 📦 Page 2: Product & Sales Analysis

Deep dive into product performance, price vs. profit analysis, and gender/category breakdowns.

### 🥇 Top Products by Profit
- Horizontal bar chart ranking products by profitability.
- Top performers: Men's Street Footwear and Women's Apparel.

### 💰 Price vs. Profit Analysis
- Scatter plot illustrating the relationship between unit price and operating margin.
- Examples: Men's Apparel and Men's Footwear show stable margins within certain price ranges.
